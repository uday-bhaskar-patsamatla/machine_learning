The landscape of regression modelling in 2025 and 2026 is shifting toward explainable AI (XAI), real-time edge computing, and multi-modal data integration. While classic problems like house price prediction remain valuable for beginners, advanced practitioners are now focusing on sustainability, personalized healthcare, and automated software quality. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 
## Sustainability & Climate Tech

* EV Charging Demand Forecasting: Predicting hourly demand per charging station to optimize grid load and dynamic pricing.
* Green Hydrogen Storage Behavior: Using regression to predict hydrogen absorption characteristics (valence electron concentration and electronegativity) in high-entropy alloys.
* Carbon Footprint Prediction: Estimating real-time emissions for low-carbon dual-fuel engines under varying operating conditions.
* Smart Water Management: Predicting potential water-borne disease outbreaks by correlating symptoms with IoT-based water quality reports (turbidity, pH). [6, 7, 8] 

## Healthcare & Personalized Medicine

* Real-time Wearable Vitals: Developing regression models that run locally on low-power wearables to predict recovery milestones or health anomalies.
* Non-Invasive Glucose Sensing: Estimating blood glucose levels using microwave-based sensor data and deep neural networks.
* Maternal Health Risk Assessment: Predicting pregnancy-related risks by analyzing physiological data and life-stage-specific hormonal changes.
* Disease Progression Risk: Forecasting the likelihood of Gestational Diabetes (GDM) escalating to Type 2 Diabetes. [3, 9, 10, 11, 12] 

## Fintech & Global Markets

* RBI-Compliant Credit Scoring: Building transparent regression models that meet the latest regulatory norms for lending and credit risk assessment.
* Global Ad-Spend ROI: Analyzing the correlation between diverse advertising expenditures (TV, social media, radio) and sales across multiple international markets.
* Cryptocurrency Volatility: Using time-series regression and LSTMs to forecast Bitcoin and altcoin price movements based on historical volume and macroeconomic indicators. [3, 12, 13, 14, 15] 

## Software Engineering & Logistics

* AI-Driven Regression Testing: Using AI to select and prioritize software test cases after code changes, shrinking regression cycles from hours to minutes.
* Last-Mile Delivery "Time-to-Door": Calculating precise delivery times on handsets for high-density urban areas using real-time traffic and location data.
* Automated Cost Estimators: Predicting vehicle damage repair costs for insurance claims using image-extracted features and regression analysis. [3, 4, 12] 

## Advanced Methodologies to Explore

* Explainable ML (SHAP/LIME): Transitioning from "black box" models to interpretable hybrid architectures.
* Modal & Quantile Regression: Moving beyond predicting the "mean" to address measurement errors, spatial data, and high-dimensional covariates.
* Regularization Techniques: Applying Lasso, Ridge, and Elastic Net to genomics or high-dimensional datasets to prevent overfitting. [2, 15, 16, 17, 18]

## Sustainability & Climate Tech## 1. EV Charging Demand Forecasting
Predict hourly energy demand at public charging stations to optimize grid load and dynamic pricing.

* Key Features: Time of day, seasonality, proximity to traffic hubs, weather, and real-time grid capacity.
* Resources: [IEA Global EV Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2025), [UrbanEV Open Benchmark Dataset](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.np5hqc04z).

## 2. Green Hydrogen Storage Behavior
Predict hydrogen absorption characteristics in high-entropy alloys (HEAs) for efficient storage.

* Key Features: Valence electron concentration, electronegativity, and atomic size difference.
* Resources: [IEA Hydrogen Tracker](https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-tools/hydrogen-tracker), [Global Hydrogen Compass 2025](https://compass.hydrogencouncil.com/).

## 3. Carbon Footprint for Dual-Fuel Engines
Estimate real-time CO2 emissions for low-carbon engines under varying load and fuel-blend conditions.

* Key Features: Engine displacement, fuel type (Hydrogen/Ethanol), RPM, and exhaust temperature.
* Resources: [Vehicle CO2 Emissions Dataset (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/brsahan/vehicle-co2-emissions-dataset), [Low-Carbon Engine Performance Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/programmer3/low-carbon-engine-performance-data).

## 4. Smart Water Management & Disease Prediction
Correlate IoT water quality metrics (pH, turbidity) with the likelihood of water-borne disease outbreaks.

* Key Features: Dissolved oxygen, temperature, conductivity, and regional health reports.
* Resources: [Nature: IoT Water-Related Disease System](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-79989-6).

## 6. Non-Invasive Glucose Sensing
Estimate blood glucose levels using microwave-based sensor data and deep neural networks.

* Key Features: Microwave signal attenuation, phase shift, and physiological baseline data.

## 7. Maternal Health Risk Assessment
Predict pregnancy-related risks by analyzing hormonal changes and life-stage-specific physiological data.

* Key Features: Blood pressure, age, blood sugar, and historical risk factors.
* Resources: Maternal Health Risk Data (UCI).
